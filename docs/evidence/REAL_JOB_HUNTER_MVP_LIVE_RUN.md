## Pipeline

1. **Live search acquisition** — 7 focused Google queries (via the proven
   SerpApi integration from JH-SUP-0020/0021, `hl=hu`, `gl=hu`), derived from
   `profile/persona.md`'s ranked target positions:
   `IT vezető állás Budapest`, `informatikai vezető állás Budapest`,
   `IT osztályvezető állás Budapest`, `infrastruktúra vezető állás Budapest`,
   `IT projektmenedzser állás Budapest`, `digitalizációs vezető állás Budapest`,
   `AI transzformációs vezető állás Budapest`. 52 unique candidate URLs after
   dedup.
2. **Job-detail verification, not just SERP titles** — each candidate page was
   fetched and checked for `schema.org/JobPosting` structured data (the same
   markup job boards use for Google for Jobs indexing). Pages without it were
   classified as listing/category pages, and up to 6 job-like links were
   extracted from each listing for a second-level fetch. **Only pages with a
   confirmed `JobPosting` schema were treated as real, individual job
   advertisements** — 50 confirmed job-ad pages total. This directly
   satisfies "search result pages alone are not enough; inspect the actual
   listing content" and "no generic search page presented as if it were a job
   advertisement."
3. **Field extraction** from the structured schema itself (title,
   `hiringOrganization`, `jobLocation`, `description`, `employmentType`,
   `datePosted`, `validThrough`) — not guessed from free text. Where a field
   could not be found, it is recorded as `unknown`, never invented.
4. **Hard-exclusion gate**, in order: (a) position relevance — title must
   contain an IT-management/leadership keyword and not a clearly unrelated
   domain (recruiting, marketing, customer service); (b) excluded company
   (`profile/exclusions.yaml`); (c) advanced/fluent/negotiation-level/
   native-level English requirement (current persona rule — intermediate/basic
   English is explicitly **not** disqualifying).
5. **Transparent scoring**: base weight from which persona-priority query
   found the role (IT vezető/informatikai vezető highest, AI-transformation
   lowest), +10 for Budapest/agglomeration, +5 for remote/hybrid, +15 for
   explicit management-scope language in the description, and a documented
   deprioritization (not exclusion) for senior-IC or PM titles lacking real
   management scope, per the Product Owner's own refinement to
   `profile/learned_preferences.md`.

## Language-requirement gate — demonstrated working

One candidate (**IT Manager (Production Environment)**, a recruiter-posted
LinkedIn listing) was rejected specifically for a mandatory
advanced/fluent-level English requirement in its description, confirming the
hard-exclusion gate fires correctly. Intermediate/basic English mentions found
elsewhere did not trigger rejection, matching the current persona rule.

## Rejection summary

- **39** rejected as not position-relevant (title lacked an IT-leadership
  keyword, or matched an excluded domain like recruiting/marketing/customer
  service) — e.g. *IT Rendszerszervező* (Alfa Vienna Insurance, not a
  management title), *Employer Brand Manager* (NIX, HR not IT), *Junior IT
  Toborzó* (NIX, recruiting).
- **1** rejected for mandatory advanced English.
- **0** rejected for the excluded-companies list (`BadCompanyKft` did not
  appear among live candidates this run).
- **80** second-level and first-level candidate pages were unreachable or
  could not be classified (fetch errors, no `JobPosting` schema and too few
  job-like links to treat as a listing) — mostly non-job-board pages picked
  up incidentally by the search (e.g. news articles, generic company pages).
  These were excluded rather than guessed at.
